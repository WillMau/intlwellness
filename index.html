The idea

Wellness was never meant to live in ten different apps and five different buildings. We brought it back to one calm place.

Most people don't fail at being well because they lack willpower. They fail because the support is scattered: a gym here, a therapist there, a nutrition app that nags. INTL Wellness closes the gap. Your training, your stillness practice, your coaching, and your care team share the same hallway and the same notes on you.

A week at INTL Wellness

A rhythm, not a to-do list.

Four guided 30-minute sessions a week, an hour of coaching every other week, and a care team you can reach in between. Around four and a half hours that quietly reorganize the other one hundred and sixty-three.

  1. 01

    Strength, twice a week

    Two 30-minute coached sessions in the gym at Crunch, programmed for you, never improvised.

    2 × 30 min · weekly
  2. 02

    Yoga & movement

    A guided practice to mobilize, lengthen, and undo what sitting all week does to a body.

    30 min · weekly
  3. 03

    Guided meditation

    Thirty minutes of trained stillness: breath, attention, and a nervous system that finally exhales.

    30 min · weekly
  4. 04

    One-on-one coaching

    A full hour with your coach to look at the whole picture and adjust the plan to your real life.

    60 min · every other week

Your care team

Four pillars, one set of people who talk to each other.

Movement

A movement specialist and strength coaches who build the body you'll want in twenty years, not just this summer.

Stillness

Yoga and guided meditation that treat the nervous system as seriously as the muscles. Rest as a discipline.

Nourishment

A registered dietitian and nutritionist to make eating feel obvious again. No fads, no guilt, just what works for you.

Mind

Trusted referrals to licensed therapists and psychologists, so the inner work has somewhere real to go.
